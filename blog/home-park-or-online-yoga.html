Home, park or online yoga: how to choose the right class

By Kiirti Sharma24 November 20264 min read

The best yoga class is the one you'll actually keep coming back to. Choosing the right setting from the start makes that far more likely.

At Be Yogiic there are several ways to practise. Here's a simple guide to help you pick the one that fits your life.

First, ask yourself three questions

  1. What's my main goal? Flexibility, stress relief, weight management, recovery from pain, or simply feeling better?
  2. How predictable is my schedule? Fixed routines suit group classes, while busy or changing days suit private or online sessions.
  3. What keeps me motivated? Some people thrive on personal attention, others on the energy of a group.

Private classes at home

Best for: beginners, anyone with a specific health concern, and busy professionals.

Every session is designed around your body and goals, with hands-on guidance and timing that fits your day. Be Yogiic offers private yoga at home across Malad and nearby areas. It's the fastest way to build a safe foundation, and the most personal.

Park yoga

Best for: nature lovers and anyone who feels refreshed outdoors.

Practising under the trees, with fresh air and morning light, adds a calm that's hard to recreate indoors. Park sessions are held for a minimum of two people, so bring a friend or partner along.

Still not sure which is right for you? Kiirti will help you choose.

Group classes

Best for: people who love community and steady motivation.

Practising with others builds energy and accountability, and it's a lovely way to grow together with friends or family. Classes suit all levels.

Online classes

Best for: frequent travellers, people outside Mumbai, and anyone short on time.

Live, guided sessions from wherever you are, with no commute. They're available one-on-one or as a group, and are just as attentive as being in the room.

Quick guide: new to yoga or managing pain → private. Love the outdoors → park. Want company → group. Always on the move → online.

Whichever you choose, the most important step is the first one. See all packages and pricing on the home page.
