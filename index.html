Good to know

Frequently asked questions

I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?

Absolutely. Every session is personalised for your age, fitness and experience. Beginners start with the foundations: breath, alignment and simple poses, and progress at a comfortable pace.

Where do the yoga classes take place?

Private classes are held at your home in Malad and nearby areas, park yoga takes place outdoors, and group and private sessions are also available live online, so you can join from anywhere.

How much do yoga classes cost?

Monthly fees: online classes start at ₹1,700 (weekend group) and go up to ₹6,500 (private). Home visit classes range from ₹2,500 (weekend group) to ₹11,000 (private). A couple offer is also available. Get in touch to know the sessions included.

Is there a minimum group size for group or park yoga?

Yes. Group classes, park yoga and group online classes need a minimum of two people, which makes them a lovely way to practise with a friend, partner or family member.

Can yoga help with back pain or weight management?

Be Yogiic offers dedicated yoga therapy and weight management programmes designed around your goals. Many people find regular practice improves flexibility, strength, stress and sleep. If you have a medical condition, please consult your doctor first so your sessions can be adapted safely. You may also like our guides on yoga for lower back stiffness and yoga for weight loss.

What do I need for online yoga classes?

A phone, tablet or laptop with a stable internet connection, a yoga mat and enough space to stretch out. Kiirti will share the joining details when you book.

What should I wear and bring?

Comfortable clothes you can move in, a yoga mat and a bottle of water. It's best to avoid a heavy meal for about two hours before practice.

How do I book a session?

Message Kiirti on WhatsApp (the enquiry form fills in your details for you) or call +91 82910 01827. She'll get back to you to understand your goals and suggest the right package.
